## Limits

Unsigned Windows x64 only; ARM/Linux/macOS/Docker are not supported package targets. English OCR only; Office conversion, Ghostscript/unpaper cleanup, standalone qpdf and other optional helper-dependent tools require separately installed integrations. PDF fonts and multilingual document rendering resources remain bundled. No broad document-corpus fidelity benchmark, real-profile installation, Office conversion test, antivirus system, new authentication, deployment or binary-publication clearance is claimed. Full corresponding-source/build provenance for redistribution remains outstanding and is explicitly not replaced by a source offer on the user's behalf.
